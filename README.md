One complete story in exactly six words:

> She waited sixty years. Wrong station.

A lifetime of waiting, then the turn: she was waiting in the wrong
place all along. The reader fills in whom she waited for, why she
never left, and whether she ever found them.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (pure Python 3.13 standard library, no third-party packages)
writes the story to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly 6 words, 38
characters, byte-identical on every run.
